## Editorial and Community Policies

The Genomics × AI blog follows transparent, Git-native policies for submissions, review, and discussion.

### Submission and Review

- Posts are currently **by invitation** from the editorial team. We plan to open submissions to everyone once we have a pool of reviewers in place, and will announce it in [Announcements](https://github.com/genomicsxai/genomicsxai.github.io/discussions/categories/announcements) and on social media.  
- All posts are submitted via **Pull Request** and require **at least one editor approval** before merge.  
- Authors are expected to complete lab-internal review before submitting.  
- Editors review for suitability, scientific accuracy, and adherence to [Submission Guidelines](/submission-guidelines/).  

### Access and Permissions

- **Authors**: Submit and revise posts via PRs.  
- **Editors**: Review, request changes, and merge to `main`. Implemented via GitHub branch protection and the [@genomicsxai/editors](https://github.com/orgs/genomicsxai/teams/editors) team (CODEOWNERS).  
- **Readers**: View content and participate in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/genomicsxai/genomicsxai.github.io/discussions).  

### License and copyright

- Posts are published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Anyone may share and adapt a post, provided they credit its authors and link to it or cite its DOI.  
- **Authors keep their copyright.** The license covers the post's text and figures, its PDF and its Zenodo record, except third-party material marked otherwise in its caption (see below). Code repositories linked from a post keep their own licenses.  
- By submitting, you confirm you have the rights to publish the content under this license and that it meets our [Submission Guidelines](/submission-guidelines/).  

### Figures and other third-party material

Cite the source of every figure you did not make, in its caption and in the References section. Whether you can include it depends on its license:

- **CC BY or CC0**: reuse or adapt it, and give the license in the caption, e.g. *"Adapted from Smith et al. [3], CC BY 4.0."*  
- **CC BY-NC or CC BY-ND** (or both): include it, unchanged if it is ND, and state in the caption that it is under its own license and not covered by the post's CC BY license.  
- **Free to read but not openly licensed** (the publisher holds copyright): you need the publisher's permission. Making your own figure from the underlying data, or linking to the original, is usually easier.  
- **Your own figures**: reuse them freely from your own preprints, where authors normally keep copyright. For a figure from your published article, check what your copyright agreement with the journal allows.  

### Prior and dual publication

- A post can build on your own preprint, paper, talk, lab blog or newsletter, and there is no embargo or required delay either way.  
- The post here should be **distinct content**, written or adapted for this blog's readers, not a copy-paste of a version published elsewhere. Give the other version's link in your pull request so editors can compare the two.  
- Link the two versions to each other. The version here is archived with a Zenodo DOI and published for Google Scholar, so it is the one to cite: please cite it by DOI from the other version.  

### Changes to These Policies

Policy updates are proposed and discussed via the repository (e.g. issues or discussions) and reflected in this page after consensus.
